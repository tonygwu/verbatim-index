# Secondary sources are unreliable in THREE distinct ways. All three found in one session.

## 1. Outlets manufacture commitment (G3)
Novogratz: headline AND video title say "$40,000 bitcoin by end of 2018".
  Captions: "Bitcoin COULD be at 40,000 at the end of the 2018 it really easily could"
Thiel: circulating quote "I am still hopeful that bitcoin goes up by a factor of 100x".
  Captions: "I'm still hopeful that IF uh Bitcoin goes up by a factor of 100 you'll
  make some money a modest amount of money in real terms"
  => The article turned a SUBORDINATE IF-CLAUSE into the prediction itself.
     He is not forecasting 100x. He is saying what your real return would be if.
Hinton: article "we've got plenty of radiologists"; captions "we got plenty".

## 2. Outlets DELETE the speaker's own disclaimer (G3)
Khosla, TechCrunch Disrupt 2013. Article prints the 10-year data-science claim.
Captions show he immediately adds: "Now, that's a pretty stunning statement.
If I'm right, I'm often wrong." Invisible in every article. New G3 risk.
Also his 80% line in full: "I've BEEN ON THE RECORD SAYING 80% of what doctors
do CAN BE DONE..." = a report of a past statement about a present capability.
Fails G1 AND G2. The article's phrasing hid both. Lead downgraded to rejected.

## 3. *** Transcript aggregators MISATTRIBUTE SPEAKERS (G4) ***
THE MOST IMPORTANT FINDING. podscripts.co attributed an All-In E141 monologue to
Bill Gurley. Captions prove it is BRAD GERSTNER. Three markers in the same
monologue: "I'm in the bill Gurley Camp" (nobody says that about himself);
"altimeter will compete for those IPOs" (Altimeter is Gerstner's fund);
"I just returned from Deer Valley".
=> Bill Gurley fell from 4 past-due leads to 1. Three rows withdrawn.
The agent had rated them HIGH confidence.

CAUSE: multi-host show + auto-captions with NO SPEAKER LABELS + a summarising
model that guessed attribution from the episode TITLE.
This is the same failure as the Jensen-Huang-under-Andrew-Ng trap (file 02):
right recording, wrong speaker.

MITIGATION THAT WORKED: prefer episodes where each claim is introduced BY NAME
("sacks, you got a 2024 prediction of worst performing asset"). That cue is what
made E110/E160 safe and its absence is what broke E141.
BONUS: All-In annual episodes OPEN by scoring last year's calls aloud, naming
who made each one. Free attribution check AND a resolution hint on one page.
